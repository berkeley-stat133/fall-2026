Your task is to understand the basic structure of a new data set from 2020 related to tuition costs at colleges and universities across the country. It is stored as a csv on github that can be read into your R session (or a Quarto document) using:

tuition_cost <- readr::read_csv('https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rfordatascience/tidytuesday/main/data/2020/2020-03-10/tuition_cost.csv')

For each of the following, please answer the question in full sentences, provide the code that you used, along with the plot (or a sketch of it).

  1. What are the dimensions of the data set? What is the unit of observation? What is the nature of the variables (what they are, their units or levels, and their type)?
  1. Is there any missing data? If so, where?
  1. What is the distribution of the cost of in-state tuition? Answer with summary statistics, a plot, and a written description of its shape.
  1. The type variable is intended to sort institutions into three types: public, private, and for-profit. Evaluate whether that is true of this data set and let it inform your answer to the next question.
  1. How does the distribution of in-state tuition differ between the types of institutions? Answer with a plot and a written description of how the distributions compare.
  1. Which are the three most expensive institutions? The three least expensive institutions?
  1. Create one more visualization of at least two variables that you’re curious about. Describe the structure that you see with a written description. Then write down one new question that your plot raises, one that you would need more digging (or more data) to answer.
